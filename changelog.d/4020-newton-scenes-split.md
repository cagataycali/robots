### Docs: the Newton page keeps the backend, the live scene gets its own page

`docs/simulation/newton.md` had grown to 2,794 words by carrying two subjects:
what the backend is - install, solvers, parity, URDF robots - and everything a
live scene does. The second half is now
`docs/simulation/newton-scenes.md`: discovery and per-joint state queries, the
interactive viewer, LeRobotDataset recording, per-episode randomization and
sensor noise, and mesh objects. Both pages are under the 1,500-word budget, and
the nav and the deep link from the randomization page point at the section's new
home.
