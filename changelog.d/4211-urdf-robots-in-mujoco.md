### Added: every robot_descriptions URDF-only robot loads in the MuJoCo backend (81 more robots)

`Robot("atlas_v4", mode="sim")`, `Robot("ur10")`, `Robot("anymal_d")` and 78
other names that robot_descriptions ships only as URDF now build in MuJoCo.
`strands_robots/assets/urdf.py` compiles the URDF on first use into a
Menagerie-shaped asset (`robot.xml`, `scene.xml`, meshes) under
`~/.strands_robots/assets/<module>/`: mesh URIs resolved (`package://`,
relative), Collada converted with trimesh, the inertials, zero-size geoms and
duplicate materials MuJoCo refuses repaired, one position actuator per joint
sized from the URDF effort, a free joint for floating bases, the root lifted
onto the floor, a floor and a light. The 23 xacro-only descriptions render
through xacrodoc. `registry/urdf_robots.json` (generated by
`scripts/build_urdf_registry.py`) records each robot's compiled joint count and
upstream pin; `list_robots()` reports them with `source: "urdf"` (156 robots,
145 with sim), `get_robot`/`has_sim` follow, and the docs give each a page and
a thumbnail plus `learn/simulation/urdf.md`. New extra `[sim-urdf]` (trimesh,
pycollada, xacrodoc). 80 of 81 build; `eve_r3` is listed with the reason its
upstream repository no longer exists.
