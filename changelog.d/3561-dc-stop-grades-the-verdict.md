### Fixed: `robot_mesh(action="stop")` over Device Connect reported a refused stop as a success

A device answers its `stop` RPC with an envelope, and an authorization refusal or a `stop_policy` that could not halt a rollout arrives as a delivered reply rather than a raised call. The single-target `stop` branch counted delivery as a stop, so the refusal was returned under `status="success"` and its audit row recorded `ok=True` - an agent branching on the envelope proceeded as if the arm had halted, and the audit log agreed with it. The branch now reads the reply with the same rule the fleet-wide `emergency_stop` branch and the built-in mesh path use: a device that reports it did not stop turns the result into an error naming the target and its answer, the audit row records the refusal, and it is logged at CRITICAL. A reply that is not an envelope is still not a failure report.
