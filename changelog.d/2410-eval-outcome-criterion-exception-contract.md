### Fixed

- **simulation**: an episode-outcome criterion that raises is now reported instead of propagating. Both eval routes call a caller-supplied criterion after every applied action (`eval_policy`'s `success_fn`, a benchmark spec's `is_success` / `is_failure`), and every other per-step hook on those paths already stated what a raise means - `on_frame` is best-effort telemetry, `spec.on_step` returns `status="error"` naming the hook, `run`'s `stop_when` is fatal with a message naming the step. The outcome criterion had no such policy, and neither `PolicyRunner.evaluate` nor `PolicyRunner._evaluate_with_spec` carried the terminal handler `PolicyRunner.run` has, so a criterion that raised left the method as the caller's own exception: `eval_policy` returned a traceback rather than a structured result, and every completed episode was discarded with nothing naming the criterion, the episode or the step. The failure is now `status="error"` carrying all three plus `episodes_completed`. Verdicts are coerced with `bool()` rather than type-checked, so the `numpy.bool_` an ordinary predicate returns is still accepted.
