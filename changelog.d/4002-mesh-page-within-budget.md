### Docs: the mesh guide keeps the contracts, and the wire reference gets its own page

`docs/mesh.md` was 4,148 words and two documents in one scroll - joining, e-stop,
resume and teleop, alongside everything a peer publishes. The rationale
paragraphs that restated the `strands_robots.mesh` docstrings are gone, and the
wire reference moved to `docs/mesh-topics.md`: the topic table, the `degraded`
probe block, the `active` flag, peer-record ageing and rejoining. Every contract
either page stated is kept, with the resume knobs and their defaults now a table.
The guide is 1,493 words and the new page 931, so neither is exempted from the
1,500-word page budget.
