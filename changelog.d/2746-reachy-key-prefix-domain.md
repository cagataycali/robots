### Fix: `ReachyMiniDriver` refuses a Zenoh key prefix that does not address one robot

`prefix` is the Wireless variant's whole namespace. It is interpolated verbatim into the three key expressions the robot lives on -- `<prefix>/joint_positions` and `<prefix>/imu_data` in `ZenohLink.start`, and `<prefix>/command` in `ZenohLink.send_cmd` -- and nothing downstream narrowed it, so two unusable shapes reached the wire and failed in opposite directions. Zenoh reads `*` and `**` as key-expression wildcards and accepts them on a publisher key as readily as on a subscriber one, so a wildcard prefix widened the command key from one robot's inbox into a match-any key: measured over a live session, a driver built with `prefix="reachy_mini/*"` published one `body(yaw=90)` to `reachy_mini/*/command` and both `reachy_mini/robot_a` and `reachy_mini/robot_b` received it, with the widened call reporting success. Conversely the shapes Zenoh does refuse -- an empty segment, a stray `?`, a leading or doubled `/` -- were not refused here either; they surfaced from inside the transport at `ZenohLink.start`, after `connect()` had already probed the daemon and logged a connection, rather than from the constructor call that named them. A non-string was not refused at all: `prefix=None` namespaced the robot under the literal segment `None`.

`strands_robots.mesh.security.validate_mesh_identifier` is the shared domain for one key-expression segment, and it exists for exactly this reason -- its docstring records that an unvalidated segment "silently widens a point-to-point subscription into a match-any one". A prefix is a `/`-joined sequence of those segments, so each one now goes through it, beside the existing `api_port` guard and before any base-class state is allocated or any daemon is reached. The `/` itself stays legitimate, so namespacing two Minis apart as `reachy_mini/robot_a` and `reachy_mini/robot_b` is unaffected: it is the wildcard that is refused, not multi-instance use. The accepted domain is a strict subset of what Zenoh will carry, so nothing the constructor admits can reach the late-refusal path, and the refusal names the class, the parameter and the offending segment.
