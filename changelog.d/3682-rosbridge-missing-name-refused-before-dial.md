### Fixed: `use_rosbridge` refuses a call missing its required name before dialing the bridge. A `publish` or `service_call` without `type`, or an `echo` without `topic`, used to open the WebSocket first, so with no bridge listening you waited out the connect timeout and were told the bridge "did not reconnect", and without roslibpy you were told to install it. The missing name is now reported at once, identically with or without a reachable bridge, alongside the numeric-option checks.
