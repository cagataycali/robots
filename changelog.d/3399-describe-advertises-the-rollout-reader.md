### Fixed

- `SimEngine.describe()` now advertises `list_policies_running` on every backend. The verb was promoted onto the ABC so it answers everywhere, but its `describe()` entry stayed in the MuJoCo override, so on Newton and Isaac the verb returned `status="success"` while the discovery surface a caller enumerates did not name it -- and the base `start_policy` entry told that caller a backend "advertises `list_policies_running` beside it". The entry text now has one owner (`LIST_POLICIES_RUNNING_DESCRIBE_ENTRY`), read by the base surface and by the one backend that assembles its own `methods` mapping, so a second copy cannot drift from the implementation again. MuJoCo's own override is dropped: its wording described "a background policy", which the shared population has not been limited to since it began counting a blocking `run_policy` rollout as well.
