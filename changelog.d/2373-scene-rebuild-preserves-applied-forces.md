### Fixed
- **Simulation (MuJoCo)**: a force latched with `apply_force` is no longer silently revoked when the scene is rebuilt. Both rebuild paths dropped the two applied-force buffers - `spec.recompile` (used by `add_robot`, `add_object` and `add_camera`) transfers `qpos`/`qvel`/`ctrl`/`act` and the clock but neither `qfrc_applied` nor `xfrc_applied`, and `remove_robot`'s fresh `MjData` zero-fills both while its name-keyed restore carried only the joint-space half. A 1 kg pod held against gravity by a 9.81 N wrench fell 0.329 m the moment a crate was added, under a `"status": "success"`, contradicting `add_robot`'s documented promise that "a latched `apply_force` wrench persists". Both buffers are now restored by name - body name for `xfrc_applied`, joint key for `qfrc_applied` - from a single owner shared by the two paths.
