### Fixed

- **simulation/ik**: `discover_ee_frame()` matched its end-effector hint words as bare substrings, so the short hints fired inside unrelated words - `"ee"` occurs in `"knee"`, `"wheel"` and `"unitree"`. Because `"ee"` precedes `"wrist"` in the body-hint order, a leg link outranked the arm, and 12 of the 62 loadable registry robots resolved a knee or a drive wheel as their end-effector: the frame `move_to()` servos toward and every `eef_delta` / `cartesian_delta` policy chunk is applied to. Hints now match name *components* - names are split into tokens on separators, camelCase boundaries and letter/digit boundaries, and a hint matches when its own tokens occur as a consecutive run - so `attachment_site`, `wristYawLeft`, `tool0` and `ee_link` all still resolve while a hint can no longer fire inside an unrelated word.
