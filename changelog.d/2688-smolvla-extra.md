### Fixed

- **packaging**: SmolVLA is a first-class `lerobot_local` policy type (`resolution.py` lists it, `policy.py` imports `transformers` for its tokenizer), but no extra installed its dependencies. The base `[lerobot]` extra is `lerobot[feetech,dataset]`, which pulls no transformers/num2words/accelerate, so `pip install 'strands-robots[lerobot]'` and then running SmolVLA failed at first use (the SmolVLM backbone processor imports `num2words`). Added a `[smolvla]` extra that defers to lerobot's own `[smolvla]` extra (`transformers-dep>=5.4.0,<5.6.0` + num2words + `accelerate-dep`), mirroring how `[molmoact2]` defers to `lerobot[molmoact2]` so the two share one transformers specifier across `[all]`. Added it to `[all]` and to the `vera-sim` conflict set, and pointed the training docs' smolvla row at `strands-robots[smolvla]`.
