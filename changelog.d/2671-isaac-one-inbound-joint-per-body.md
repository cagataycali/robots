### Fixed

- **simulation/isaac**: `_validate_kinematic_tree` states that each non-root body has exactly one inbound joint, as USD/PhysX articulations require, but it counted duplicate `(parent_body, child_body)` edges. That catches only the case where two joints share a parent - an MJCF body carrying two `<joint>` children - while a URDF is a flat list of joints each naming `<parent>` and `<child>` explicitly, so two joints can name the same child with different parents: two distinct edges, one link with two parents. Measured through `load_urdf`, a three-link arm whose `forearm` was the child of both `base` and `shoulder` loaded and reported `num_joints=3` for an articulation that can carry two, and a joint whose `parent_body` was its own `child_body` loaded too. Inbound joints are now counted per child and a self-parented joint is refused on its own terms; the duplicate-edge refusal keeps its wording and its remedy, since splitting with an intermediate massless link fixes a compound joint and is the wrong advice for a link carrying one joint too many. Several roots stay legitimate - the shipped `unitree_g1` builder has two - so connectivity and general acyclicity remain out of scope.
