### Fixed:
- `strands-robots doctor` no longer reports "no CUDA device to compare against" on a machine whose only missing piece is torch. The CUDA probe now asks the driver itself (`libcuda.so.1`, falling back to `/proc/driver/nvidia/gpus`), so a Jetson or GPU box without torch reads "torch not installed, but the driver reports 1 CUDA device(s)", both arch lines report a device as present rather than claiming there is none to compare against, a CPU-only torch next to a GPU is called out, and the remedy fits the machine: the generic command on x86_64 and on JetPack 7 (PyPI's aarch64 torch carries CUDA from 2.11), NVIDIA's `jp6/cu126` index on JetPack 6, whose 12.6 driver cannot load it.
