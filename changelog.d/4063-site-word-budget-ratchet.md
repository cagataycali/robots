### Docs: the word budget grades the whole site, not only the page

A page coming inside the 1,500-word per-page ceiling does not make the site
shorter: a split pays a front matter, a nav row and a see-also block, so the
rule is satisfied by moving words rather than removing them. Over the last 22
pages added, `docs/` grew from 113,472 to 113,954 words while the number of
pages named as owing a split fell from 25 to 2, and nothing graded the
difference. `_SITE_BUDGET` now grades the site the way the per-page rule grades
the page, and only ever moves down - a change that adds words pays for them
with a cut, and a change that cuts words lowers the ceiling so the room it
freed cannot be spent unnoticed.

The security pages pay the first step: `security.md`, `security/commands.md`,
`security/hardware.md`, `security/mesh.md` and `security/audit-log.md` lose the
derivation stated beside the rule it justifies - how a CR in an `instruction`
would forge a second audit record, that a clamped `[1e6, 0, 0]` is still a
sprint the caller did not command, which parent path a leading `.` resolves to,
and the static scan that backs the closed refusal vocabulary. Every rule,
refused input, quoted refusal, environment variable and threat statement stays,
and the site is 113,660 words rather than 113,954.
