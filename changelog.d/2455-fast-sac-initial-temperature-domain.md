### Fixed

- **training/rl**: FastSAC now preflights `init_alpha`, the SAC entropy temperature's starting value, against the same positive-finite domain as the `alpha_lr` that moves it. The trainer stores the temperature's logarithm, so `init_alpha=0` gave `log(0) = -inf` and a temperature of exactly zero - the entropy term absent from both the critic target and the actor loss, unrecoverable by `autotune_alpha` because no finite gradient step moves an infinity - while the run reported success and checkpointed the non-finite value; a negative value, `nan` or `inf` instead raised from inside `torch.distributions.Normal` about a tensor of `nan` policy means, naming that distribution's parameter rather than the field, once `train()` was already under way. Unlike `alpha_lr` the check is not conditioned on `autotune_alpha`, because the field is read on both branches.
