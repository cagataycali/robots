### Tests: `BoosterDriver.rotate_head` is covered. The T1's head sits outside `UPPER_BODY_SLOTS`, so `send_action` refuses the two head slots and points at `rotate_head()` -- making it the only path to the head, and it had no test at all. `RotateHead` takes the angles as C++ doubles and returns no outcome, so the driver's own refusals are the whole contract: a non-finite `pitch`/`yaw` is now pinned as refused before the call, an unconnected driver as a named refusal rather than an `AttributeError`, a throwing client as an error envelope, and an accepted call as widened to `float` and echoed back.
