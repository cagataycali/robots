### Fixed
- `create_policy("lerobot_local", ..., rtc_enabled=True)` now engages Real-Time Chunking on public flow-matching checkpoints (`lerobot/smolvla_base`, Pi0, Pi0.5), which all ship `config.rtc_config = None`: the provider constructs an `RTCConfig` from the caller's `rtc_execution_horizon` / `rtc_max_guidance_weight` (lerobot's defaults otherwise) and calls lerobot's `init_rtc_processor()`. It used to warn `has no rtc_config` and silently fall back to `select_action()`. Inference also runs under `torch.no_grad` instead of `torch.inference_mode`, so lerobot's RTC guidance can take the gradient it needs - the second chunk used to die with "element 0 of tensors does not require grad". ACT and Diffusion, whose configs declare no `rtc_config` field, keep the warning and the `select_action()` path.
