### Docs: the Kimodo page keeps the policy, sampling and chaining get their own

`docs/policies/kimodo.md` was 1,847 words. Four passages measured a rule rather
than stating it - a walking clip traced to the floor, 600 ordered pairs of a
25-motion corpus, a fractional seed keyed as `int(seed)`, the truthiness behind
the `trust_remote_code` refusal - and what remained was two subjects. The page is
1,397 words and keeps the policy, its install, its config fields and the
native-runtime adapter; when the sampler runs again, the seed contract and
chained-prompt easing are a new `docs/policies/kimodo-sampling.md` at 482 words.
