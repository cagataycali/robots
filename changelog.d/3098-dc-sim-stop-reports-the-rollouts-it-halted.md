The Device Connect `stop` RPC on a simulation now reports which rollouts it halted instead of answering a fixed "All policies stopped". It routes each robot through the simulation's own `stop_policy` and reads the verdict that verb now carries in its `json` block, so an idle simulation, a halted rollout and a world already torn down are three different answers, a refusal is named under `not_stopped`, and a scene teardown racing the stop loop is answered rather than raised past the dispatcher.
