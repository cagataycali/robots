### Fixed

- **training/rl**: FastSAC now preflights `target_entropy`, the constant its entropy temperature is optimized *toward*, against the signed `finite_number_error` domain the on-policy loss weights already read - reached through a new `Trainer._target_entropy_problems` gate. It was the third and last caller-supplied field of the temperature block and the only one nothing judged: `init_alpha` and `alpha_lr` were both put behind a *positive*-finite domain and this field was explicitly left out of both, because it is signed by construction (it defaults to `-num_actions`) and so needed a domain neither could express. Over a 40-timestep run `validate()` returned `[]` for all eleven spellings measured. `nan`, `inf` and `-inf` made `alpha_loss` non-finite, and since `alpha` scales the entropy term of both the critic's TD target and the actor loss, the next rollout raised `ValueError` from inside `torch.distributions.Normal` about a tensor of `nan` policy means - naming that distribution's parameter rather than the field, after the env, both networks and a full rollout had been built. `target_entropy=True` was worse than a crash: `bool` is an `int` subclass, so it is a target entropy of `+1.0` where the field's own default is `-6.0` for that env, and the run reported `status="success"` while checkpointing `log_alpha == -0.0018031001091003418` against the honored run's `-0.001801646314561367`. A list or a dict raised `TypeError` out of the `float()` coercion in `setup`. `None` is exempt from the domain rather than refused by it - unlike the two `float`-annotated neighbours this field is annotated `float | None`, and its `None` is the documented request for the heuristic - and the check is not conditioned on `autotune_alpha`, because the coercion that reads the field is unconditional and a non-real value raises in `setup` on either branch. A numeric string is now refused where `float()` used to coerce it, which makes the three fields of one block agree on the spellings a config may use: `alpha_lr="3e-4"` was already rejected by name.
