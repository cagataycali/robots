### Fixed: a robot IoT certificate obeys fleet stops by default and may originate one only when designated

`provision_robot` now defaults `allow_estop_publish` to `False`, attaching `strands-robot-no-estop`: the robot subscribes to and obeys `strands/safety/estop` and `strands/safety/resume` but cannot publish on them, so one extracted certificate can no longer halt the whole fleet, arm an MQTT Will that halts it, or clear a lockout a human engaged. A designated safety authority opts in with `allow_estop_publish=True` (CLI `--estop-publish`, replacing `--no-estop-publish`). The Fleet Provisioning template attaches the obey-only policy to every zero-touch device and bootstrap creates it, and `_ensure_policy` treats a non-text policy document as differing instead of raising (f010).
